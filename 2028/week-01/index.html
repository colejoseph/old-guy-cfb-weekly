The opening-week ledger

With no Week 0 recap to settle, this edition starts where the season starts: the Week 1 slate. Six captured user games were already final when the weekly report was assembled, including two user-v-user matchups and four games against computer-controlled opponents.

The headline belongs to Washington State. The Cougars’ 30-point win at USF pairs neatly with the other verified early-season numbers around Isaac’s team: 486 yards per game on offense, and quarterback D. Lindsey sitting atop the national passing board with 699 yards and five touchdowns.

Wyoming and Stanford provided a different kind of opener. The No. 3 Cowboys [Karl_Hungus] won 20–17 at No. 4 Stanford [Todd], a three-point finish between two of the highest-ranked user teams in the current Media Poll.
